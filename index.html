Course through LIC · mile 13.1

Off the Pulaski Bridge onto 11th St/Jackson Ave → 48th Ave → Vernon Blvd → 10th St → 44th Dr → Hunter St → Crescent St → Queens Plaza South → Queensboro Bridge lower level (miles ~15–16) → First Ave.

LIC pass times are Hark's estimate, not official.

Watch near home

  • RUN LIC cheer zone, Vernon Blvd & 47th Rd, 9am–1pm. Closest.
  • Queens side of the Pulaski, at Jackson Ave/11th St.
  • 44th Dr & 21st St: Achilles and November Project cheer zones.
  • No spectators on any bridge.

LIC bar parties

  • Fifth Hammer marathon party, 9am–noon: pandesal pop-up, sign-making.
  • LIC Bar free breakfast bar from 9am, with a brass band.
  • Michelob Ultra cheer bars: Gantry Bar, Woodbines, PJ Leahy's.

Getting around

  • Subways run a normal weekend schedule. No planned work on the 7, G, E, M, N, W or F.
  • From home: 7 to Manhattan, E from Court Sq, G to Brooklyn, NYC Ferry from Hunters Point South. You likely reach Vernon-Jackson and the ferry without crossing the course; check the map.
  • Buses are detoured and can't cross Fifth Ave during the race.
  • Crowd control at Queensboro Plaza and busy UWS stations; stairways at 59, 66, 72 and 81 St may close.
  • Avoid the UWS/Columbus Circle and the East 60s–90s near First/Fifth unless you're cheering.

I · Spooky's Downtown Stroll

Manhattan 🐕 dog-friendly Free to $

For you, Tiffany and Spooky: a flea market, the Hudson waterfront, two dog runs and a Village sunset, all off the marathon course.

No dogs on the High Line, Little Island or in museums; on the subway he rides in a bag.

Timed itinerary
  • 8:30 Morning walk with Spooky in Hunters Point South Park. Center Blvd is off the course. Dress for a low near 38°F.
  • 10:00 · 7 train to 34 St–Hudson Yards with Spooky in a carry bag (MTA requires pets in a bag or carrier), or Uber/Lyft Pet. NYC Ferry now allows leashed dogs (kept within 2 ft), but its stops are on the East Side.
  • 10:30 Chelsea Flea, 29 W 25th St, 8am–5pm, dog-friendly
  • Walk west
  • 11:45 Chelsea Waterside Dog Park, 11th Ave & W 23rd St: small- and large-dog areas, 6am–1am (cleaning 10–11am and 2–3pm)
  • 12:30 Stroll south on the Hudson River Park esplanade (leashed, dogs off the lawns). See Little Island from outside; dogs aren't allowed in. Stop at Gansevoort Peninsula Dog Park.
  • 1:30 Lunch at a dog-friendly patio in the Meatpacking/West Village
  • 2:30 Washington Square Park (leashed dogs welcome; it has a dog run), then wander SoHo
  • 4:30 Pier 45 at Christopher St for the 4:52 sunset. Leroy Street Dog Park is next door.
  • Home: Uber/Lyft Pet, or the subway with Spooky in his bag

Good to know before you go.

Closed or not on Sunday

  • Smorgasburg is closed for the season; last market was Oct 31.
  • Queens Night Market ended Oct 31.
  • LIC Flea has no 2026 season.
  • Grand Army Plaza, Fort Greene, Inwood and the MoMA PS1 market are Saturday-only.
  • Green-Wood's big free Day of the Dead party is Monday Nov 2, 4–7pm. Sunday has only the ofrenda and a 10am tour.
  • No Knicks or Nets home games. Knicks host Nets Mon Nov 2.

Broadway deals still open

Most Sunday lotteries closed Saturday. These remain:

  • Rocky Horror $30 TodayTix lottery, until 9am Sunday
  • Paranormal Activity $40 rush, 9am
  • Maybe Happy Ending $49 digital rush, 11am
  • Stranger Things $40 box-office rush

Hours to note

  • Bryant Park Winter Village is open; shops 10am–8pm.
  • Brooklyn Botanic Garden closes 3:30pm in November.
  • Sunset is 4:52pm; put outdoor stops first.

Bringing Spooky?

  • No dogs on the High Line or Little Island (service animals only).
  • No dogs in museums or most indoor venues.
  • Subway and buses: dog must be in a bag or carrier. MTA pet rules
  • NYC Ferry: leashed dogs OK, within 2 ft. Policy updated June 2026
  • Off-leash runs: Chelsea Waterside (W 23rd), Gansevoort Peninsula, Leroy St (Pier 40), Tribeca (Pier 25/26).
